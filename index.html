About Employee Management Portal

Welcome to the CodeSphere Employee Management Portal, your one-stop place for everything people-related at CodeSphere Technologies. Here you can look up your colleagues, update your details, register new team members, explore our departments and find training resources. You can also check simple charts that show how our teams are growing and learning. Everything is in one place, so you spend less time searching and more time getting on with your work.

Notices

Annual training hours are due by 31 December. Check your progress on the Training page.
